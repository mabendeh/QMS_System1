# Information required from CHG to configure the official DunGPS system

Nothing below has been invented in the production workbooks; the fields are blank or marked "to confirm".

1. **Official pillar names.** Full name of **IM**; confirm the expansions of AM, PM, FSQ, EHS, LEAD, E&T, VO and SC; confirm whether EEM (Early Equipment Management) is in scope. The source file lists codes only.
2. **Bronze criteria detail.** For each of the 32 criteria: mandatory or optional, acceptance criteria, official required evidence, who verifies, and approved exceptions (e.g. PM maintenance org structure "except for approved exceptions").
3. **Silver and Gold criteria**, to load deployment phases 2–4.
4. **Owners.** Names, departments, positions, shifts, emails and pillar responsibilities, plus pillar leads, approvers/verifiers and escalation owners.
5. **Dates.** Program launch date, Bronze assessment target date, and step-level target dates per pillar.
6. **Weighting.** Whether CHG weights steps/requirements, and the weights.
7. **Process & equipment list.** Confirmed production areas, line/equipment names (Gemini, Nugget Line, Rolled Dough lines, …), shifts, and which deployment elements apply to each.
8. **Teams/SharePoint.** Confirmed folder structure under DunGPS → General → Shared, and the site URL for links.
9. **VO / Finance.** Savings-validation rules, the Finance approver, and the measured baseline for the Pre-Weigh Room Optimization & Relocation project.
10. **Thresholds.** Confirm or replace the default RAG tolerances (5 / 15 pts behind plan, 10% overdue share, 14-day escalation), the 80% maximum credit before verification, and the supervisor program target (2 verified projects per supervisor per period).
