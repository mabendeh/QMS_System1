# Supervisor guide

This guide covers the three things you'll do most: checking the matrix, updating a skill, and recommending someone for promotion.

## 1. Read the matrix

Open **Skills Matrix**.

- **All departments** shows one column per department. A blue cell means the person is Competent in *every* competency in that department. Click any cell to open that department's competencies.
- Click a **department tab** (for example *Mixing*) to see every competency in that department. By default only people with skills in that department are listed. Tick **Show everyone** to see the whole team.
- Use the filters to narrow the list by shift, department, job title, supervisor, skill status, level or promotion readiness. **Clear filters** resets them.
- **Compact view** fits more on a large screen for production meetings. **Print**, **Excel** and **PDF** export what you are looking at.

| Cell | Means |
|---|---|
| `NT` red | Needs training (required for their job, not shown yet) |
| `IT` yellow | In training, needs supervision |
| `TR` green | Trained, waiting for competency verification |
| `C` blue | Competent, verified to work independently |
| `—` grey | Not applicable / not assessed |
| `EXP` dark red | Expired, needs requalification |
| striped with `P` | A change is waiting for verification |

A red ◆ next to a competency name marks a **critical skill**.

## 2. Update a skill (under a minute)

**From the matrix:** open a department tab, click the person's cell, pick the new level, add a note if useful, and click **Save assessment**.

**From a tablet or phone:** open **Quick Assessment**, then:

1. Type the person's name or ID and tap them.
2. Pick the production area.
3. Pick the competency.
4. Tap the rating.
5. Add an optional comment.
6. Tap **Submit**.

You can update employees assigned to you. Trainers can update the areas they are authorized for. Production Managers can update anyone.

### Rules the system enforces

- **Trained** needs a training completion date.
- **Competent** needs:
  1. a **passed practical evaluation** recorded by a trainer, confirming the person worked independently,
  2. **evidence** (a signed checklist, photo or record) for critical skills, and
  3. **verification** by a Production Manager or Quality evaluator who did not run the practical evaluation.

  If you choose Competent, your request goes to **pending verification**. The cell shows a striped *P* and does **not** turn blue until it is verified. If something is missing, the system tells you exactly what.
- **Lowering a Competent rating** needs a note explaining why.
- If someone else changed the same skill after you opened it, you'll see **"Changed by someone else"**. Nothing is overwritten. Review the latest status and submit again if needed.

## 3. Training

Open **Training**.

- **Gaps to assign** lists red, required skills that have no training yet (critical skills first). Click **Assign**, choose an authorized trainer and a due date.
- **In progress** follows each assignment through the TWI steps: Prepare → Present → Try Out → Follow Up. Use **Start** and **Next step**.
- At Follow Up, the trainer opens the skill and records the **practical evaluation**. Then request **Competent** and an evaluator verifies it. The training closes automatically and the matrix turns blue.
- **Overdue** and **Requalification due** show what needs attention this week.

## 4. Recommend a promotion

1. Open the employee's profile. The **Career progression** panel shows the next level, each requirement, and progress (for example *3 of 4*).
2. When the system-checked requirements are met, click **Recommend for promotion**, explain what the person has demonstrated, and submit.
3. The request goes to the Production Manager, then HR, then final approval. You can follow it under **Promotion Review**, and withdraw it while it is in review.

Promotion recommendations never change pay. Time employed is never counted as proof of competence.
